Purpose: rules for `decisions/<date>-<slug>.md`. Template: `assets/decision.md`.

Frontmatter: `id` as YYYY-MM-DD-slug; `status`, accepted or `superseded by <id>`; `spec`, the spec it came from; `date`.

Title: the decision as a short statement, such as "Store wishlists on the server".

Context: the forces that made a choice necessary, in 2 to 5 lines. Options: each option in one line, with why it lost or won. Decision: one sentence. Consequences: what gets easier, what gets harder, what to watch.

A decision is written the moment a lasting choice is made, during grilling, planning or building. It is never edited once accepted. A new decision supersedes it, and only the old one's status changes. Agents read accepted decisions only. Step-by-step detail belongs in plan.md or reference/.
