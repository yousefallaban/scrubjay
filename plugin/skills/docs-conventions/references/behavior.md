Purpose: rules for `behavior/<domain>/<capability>.md`. Template: `assets/behavior-capability.md`.

Frontmatter: `capability`, as `<domain>/<capability>`.

Title: the capability as a verb phrase, such as "Save an item to the wishlist".

Body: the claims, in the claim format, 3 to 7 of them, and nothing else. Observable behavior only. Owners live in index.md, history in git, code locations in architecture/boundaries.md, and how it's built in architecture/.

Written by docs-map when a domain is mapped and by spec-ship when a spec ships, which copies the spec's claims without rewording and applies each Add, Change and Remove. Never edited by hand during a spec; a change to a claim is a Change in the spec that ships it.
