Purpose: rules for `glossary.md`. No template.

One line per term, sorted A to Z, created when the first term settles:
`- **Term**: meaning in product language. Code: \`Name\`. Avoid: other words.`
`Code` and `Avoid` are optional.

A term is written the moment it settles, during grilling or docs-map. Business rules never go in the glossary; a rule found while writing a term becomes a claim in behavior/.
