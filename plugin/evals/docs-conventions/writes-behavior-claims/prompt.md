Document what the returns capability "submit a return" does today, in our docs, as docs/behavior/returns/submit-return.md. The facts: a customer can return some items of a delivered order, within 30 days of delivery, and never more than were ordered. The refund is the price paid for the returned items with the order's discount applied. A return starts as requested, support approves it, then it is refunded.
